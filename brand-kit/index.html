01 — LOGO & WORDMARK

Identity

PRAVIN K

CLEAR SPACE

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the "P" in the wordmark on every side. Don't let other content crowd it.

MINIMUM SIZE

Monogram: 24px minimum (favicon/avatar contexts). Wordmark: never smaller than 14px cap-height.

USAGE

Monogram for avatars/favicons/tight spaces. Full wordmark for navbar, footer, and formal contexts.

DO

  • Use the gradient mark on dark or light neutral backgrounds only
  • Maintain the monogram's 18px corner radius ratio when scaling
  • Use the mono wordmark tracking exactly as shown

DON'T

  • Don't place the mark on busy photography or clashing colors
  • Don't recolor the gradient or stretch the mark non-uniformly
  • Don't add a drop shadow or outline to the wordmark
